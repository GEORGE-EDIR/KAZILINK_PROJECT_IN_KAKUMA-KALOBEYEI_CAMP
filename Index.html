LOCAL TALENT · REAL OPPORTUNITY

THE CHALLENGE

Talent is everywhere.
Access isn't.

Young people in refugee and host communities bring skills, ambition and ideas. Yet access to professional networks, mentorship, practical experience and work opportunities can be limited.

KaziLink is a proposed pilot designed to help bridge that gap by connecting people, skills and local opportunities.

THE PROPOSED PILOT

Six months. Shared progress.

A proposed six-month pilot in Kakuma Refugee Camp and Kalobeyei Integrated Settlement, Turkana County, Kenya.

6Months
50Participants
15Mentor target

Month 1: Community consultation and recruitment.

Month 2: Skills assessments and mentor orientation.

Month 3: Mentorship and participant profiles.

Month 4: Practical assignments and matching.

Month 5: Employer engagement and referrals.

Month 6: Evaluation and planning the next phase.

These are proposed planning targets, not confirmed commitments or results.

RESPONSIBLE BY DESIGN

Opportunity must be safe and fair.

Consent and privacy: Collect only necessary information and share profiles with permission.

Inclusive access: Encourage fair participation by refugee and host-community youth.

Safe experience: Set clear expectations and supervision for practical activities.

Fair participation: Clarify work conditions and compensation where applicable.

Accessibility: Consider transport, language, connectivity and offline participation.
